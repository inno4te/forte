🖥️ PC Builder Lab

Assemble a working computer. Drag a part onto its slot — or tap a part, then tap the slot. Watch the live power meter, then hit Check build. Pick a consistent platform: the CPU socket must match the motherboard, RAM type (DDR4/DDR5) must match, the cooler must fit and be strong enough, and the power supply must handle the load.
