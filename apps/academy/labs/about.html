Our mission

Closing the lab gap, one simulation at a time.

Across much of Africa, talented students study computer science, networking, biology and chemistry without ever setting foot in a well-equipped lab. Equipment is scarce, expensive and easily damaged. Innov8te Labs4Africa changes that.

How it works
1️⃣

Pick a lab

Choose from interactive labs across CS, networking, cyber security and biology — with chemistry and physics on the way.

2️⃣

Do the task

Drag, connect, troubleshoot and build. Every interaction works with touch and mouse alike.

3️⃣

Get corrected

Instant, specific feedback explains what's wrong and why — so mistakes become learning moments.

For teachers & communities

Use it in class, offline, or anywhere.

The whole platform is plain HTML+CSS+JS — no server, no database, no cost. A teacher can put it on a classroom computer, a community centre can mirror it offline with a USB stick, and anyone can host their own copy on free GitHub Pages.

A project of the Paid All Links Foundation

Innov8te Labs4Africa is created and maintained by Innocent Forteh under the Paid All Links Foundation, with a simple belief: practical learning is a right, not a privilege. If the labs help even one more student fall in love with science and technology, the project has done its job.
