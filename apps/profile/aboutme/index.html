Who We Are

Born from a burden
for Africa's leadership.

Africa is suffering under poor leadership, which has kept it stagnating — from the political sphere to the church. What began in January 2016 with 12 young students has grown into a recognized force in faith-based leadership education. In 2026 we graduate our 14th cohort and celebrate ten years of kingdom impact.

To equip the next generation to lead like Jesus — with integrity, compassion, and total commitment to Christ. Our Mission Statement

Vision & Values

AI Unlocked

4 Weeks · Smartphone Only · 86% Capstone Completion
Week 1

Foundations of Artificial Intelligence

Core AI concepts — ML, LLMs, GenAI, narrow AI vs. AGI vs. ASI — plus programming and web development with AI and an introduction to agentic AI.

Week 2

Creation, Development & Business

Building websites and apps with AI, content and media creation, and AI entrepreneurship using the Business Model Canvas and SOSTAC.

Week 3

Innovation, Architecture & Leadership

Ideation and architecture engineering, group project presentations, and leadership principles for the digital age.

Week 4

Advanced Applications & Closing

Advanced AI applications, Capstone project presentations, and graduation with certification.

Our Principal

Innocent Forteh, PMP®

Co-Founder of Team21 Academy and CEO of Practical CIG. A PMP®-certified Acquisition & Assistance Specialist with 20+ years across U.S. Government missions, the British High Commission, and international development — currently serving on the Africa CDC AVoHC Kofi Annan Scholars Program.

He spearheaded $200M+ in bilateral USAID programs and strengthened procurement systems across Cameroon, DR Congo, Namibia, Gabon, Angola, Iraq, and Haiti — delivering 14 years of top performance scores at the U.S. Embassy while mentoring multicultural teams.
