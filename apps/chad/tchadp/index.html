10 BUY-IN TACTICS | 10 TACTIQUES D'ADHÉSION
1 Show early wins visibly — post KPI results on the wall

2 Use "health sovereignty" language with the Director
Parler de "souveraineté sanitaire" avec le DG
3 Name 1-2 internal champions — let them lead workshops
Nommer des champions internes
4 NEVER blame individuals — frame gaps as system problems
Ne jamais blâmer les individus
5 Co-create SOPs — invite staff to review drafts, not just receive
Co-créer les SOP avec le personnel
6 Frame training as privilege: "You were selected for this"
Formation = privilège, pas remédiation
7 Link to career development in Africa CDC ecosystem
Lier au développement de carrière
8 Quantify problem cost: "3 stockout days = X patients missed"
Quantifier le coût des problèmes actuels
9 Celebrate milestones formally — brief ceremony when manual signed
Célébrer les jalons formellement
10 Start with the willing — results bring resistant stakeholders along
Commencer avec les personnes ouvertes

CHAD CONTEXT REMINDERS | RAPPELS CONTEXTE TCHAD
⚖️ Legal: Review Décret N°648 on public procurement in Week 1. All SOPs must comply. / Revoir le Décret N°648 la Semaine 1.
🌍 Language: Official language = French. Arabic widely spoken. All deliverables bilingual EN+FR. / Tous les livrables bilingues.
⚡ Power: Voltage 220V/50Hz. Specify stabilizers in equipment specs. / Stabilisateur requis dans les spécifications.
🌡️ Climate: Hot/humid. Specify environmental ranges in all equipment specs (15-35°C, 80% RH). / Préciser les plages environnementales.
🚁 Logistics: Limited local suppliers. Budget 8-20 week lead times for imports. Plan 60+ day stock buffers. / Délais 8-20 semaines pour les importations.
🔒 Security: Register with embassy Day 1. Check UN security alerts daily. Have contingency comms plan. / S'inscrire à l'ambassade Jour 1.
🤝 Donors: WHO, UNICEF, Global Fund, USAID likely present. Each has separate procurement rules. Map in Week 2. / Cartographier les règles des bailleurs Semaine 2.
