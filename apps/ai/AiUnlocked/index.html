🎯 Core Methodology
The CLEAR Prompting Framework
C
Context
Provide background so the AI understands your situation fully before it acts.
L
Language & Tone
Specify the communication style — professional, casual, enthusiastic, multilingual.
E
Expected Format
Describe the output structure: bullet list, table, essay, PDF, slides, or script.
A
Audience
Identify who the output is for — beginners, Gen Z, professionals, or a specific region.
R
Role / Persona
Tell the AI who to be: "You are an experienced marketing consultant with 10 years in Cameroon."
⚡ Team21 AI Commandments
01
Investigate
Understand what you are doing and why before applying any AI tool.
02
Review
Always check AI outputs for hallucinations. Keep the human in the loop.
03
Do No Harm
Use AI ethically. Do not use it for harmful, deceptive, or dangerous purposes.
