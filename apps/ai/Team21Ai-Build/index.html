▾

Most people who use AI are in a reactive relationship with it. They have a problem, ask AI for help, copy the output. Useful — but limited. An AI operator has a fundamentally different relationship with the technology.

Week 1 · Lesson 2
Advanced Prompting: AI as a Thinking Partner
4 Techniques That Transform Your AI Use
📖 22 min🧪 4 prompts to practice
1
Technique 1: Scenario Modelling
Map multiple futures from one decision
▾

Scenario modelling feeds a real decision into AI and asks it to map out multiple possible futures. Instead of "what should I do?", you ask: "if I do X, walk me through best-case, worst-case, and most-likely outcomes over 90 days."

Sample Prompt — Scenario ModellingYou are a senior strategic adviser with 20 years of experience in small business development in Central Africa. I am considering [describe your situation]. Map out three scenarios — optimistic, realistic, and pessimistic — for the next 90 days. For each, identify the top 3 risks and one specific action I should take in the first 7 days. Be direct and specific.
2
Technique 2: Devil's Advocate Prompting
Find every flaw before you commit
▾

Have AI actively argue against your plan. Before committing to a business decision, ask AI to find every flaw. Uncomfortable — but enormously valuable.

Sample Prompt — Devil's AdvocateI am about to [describe your plan]. Before I proceed, act as the most intelligent, well-informed critic of this idea. Identify every significant weakness, hidden risk, false assumption, and alternative I haven't considered. Do not soften your critique. Be comprehensive and ruthless. Then suggest the top 3 ways I could strengthen this plan.
3
Technique 3: Iterative Refinement Chains
Build conversations that get progressively better
▾

Rather than asking for a final output in one prompt, build a conversation that improves over multiple rounds. Start broad, then refine.

How to Do It — Step by Step
1
Give Claude the context and ask for a first draft or outline.
2
Review — identify what's right, missing, or tonally wrong.
3
Feed back: "The structure is good but the tone is too formal. Paragraph 3 misses the point. Revise."
4
Repeat. 3–5 iterations typically produces something excellent.
4
Technique 4: Role Stacking
An interdisciplinary team in one response
▾

Assign Claude a composite expert identity drawing on multiple perspectives simultaneously.

Sample Prompt — Role StackingYou will combine the analytical perspective of a McKinsey consultant, the communication instincts of an experienced journalist, and the practical wisdom of a Cameroonian entrepreneur who has built businesses with limited resources. From this combined perspective, review the following [plan/idea] and give me: your assessment, top 3 recommendations, and the single most important thing I am currently overlooking.

Week 1 · Lesson 3
Chain-of-Thought Prompting
Getting AI to Show Its Full Reasoning
📖 12 min🧪 Practice with a real decision
1
What is Chain-of-Thought & Why Does It Work?
Research-backed technique for complex problems
▾

Chain-of-thought prompting asks the AI to think out loud — to show its reasoning step-by-step before giving a conclusion. Research shows this produces more accurate, nuanced, and actionable responses for complex problems.

Sample Prompt — Chain-of-ThoughtBefore you give me your final answer, I want you to think through this problem step by step. Show me your reasoning at each stage. Consider what information you have, what assumptions you are making, where there is uncertainty, and what the logic is before you conclude. Only then give me your recommendation. Question: [your question here]

When to use it: Complex business decisions, strategic analysis, evaluating competing options, risk assessment. When stakes are high — always prompt for chain-of-thought reasoning.

Building Your Personal Prompt Library
Lesson 1.6 — Your most valuable professional asset
▾

A prompt library is a curated, tested collection of prompts that produce excellent results. Rather than writing from scratch every time, you pull from the library, customise the variables, and get consistent high-quality outputs immediately.

Structure each prompt entry with:

  • Prompt Name — descriptive and searchable
  • Category — Writing / Analysis / Research / Creative / Business
  • When to Use
  • Full Prompt Text — with [VARIABLES] clearly marked
  • Best Tool — Claude / Gemini / ChatGPT
  • Notes — what works, what to watch for

Store it in Notion or Google Docs. Start with the 25 Power Prompts in Appendix B of your manual — all immediately usable and professionally tested.
